## New findings

### N1. High — Google does not know `/en/` and `/ru/`; the sitemap was not refetched

- **Evidence:** URL Inspection: `/en/` and `/ru/` "URL is unknown to Google". Sitemap last downloaded 2026-07-05. gtd and poker sitemaps were downloaded on 2026-10-07, so Google does fetch sitemaps on this property. No page links to `/en/` or `/ru/` except javi itself.
- **Why it matters:** English and Russian pages cannot rank. Hreflang has no effect until Google crawls all three pages. This blocks the "Done when" of SERBITO-459.
- **Fix (owner, Search Console UI; the service account is read-only):** resubmit `https://javi.serbito.rs/sitemap.xml`. Request indexing for `/en/` and `/ru/`.
- **Done when:** the sitemap shows a download date ≥ 2026-10-07, and URL Inspection shows `/en/` and `/ru/` as indexed with self canonicals.

### N2. Medium — schema graph has weak entity signals

- `Organization.url` is the LinkedIn page; there is no `logo` and no `sameAs`.
- `SoftwareApplication` has no `offers`, so it is not eligible for the software rich result. This is correct while the price is not public.
- Fix with SERBITO-461: add an operator section on the landing, point `Organization.url` to it, add `logo` and `sameAs` (LinkedIn), add `Offer` when the price is public.

### N3. Low

- `og:title` / `twitter:title` keep the old wording («Javi — obavestite kupca kada paket stiže»), not the new `<title>`. Align them when SERBITO-461 changes the copy.
- Feature cards use H2 for short labels, and the hidden thank-you box adds «Hvala!» as an H2. The heading outline has 6 H2s and no real sections. Use H3 for cards when the page gets real sections.
- `/privacy.html` has no JSON-LD. Low value; skip unless the page gets a named controller.
