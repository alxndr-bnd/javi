**The base is clean, but Google does not see the product the way a Serbian shop sees it.**

1. **Google indexes the English version.** The landing picks its language in JavaScript from `navigator.language`. Googlebot renders with `en-US`, so the indexed title, description and H1 are English. The Serbian text exists only in the raw HTML. There are no per-language URLs, so hreflang is impossible.
2. **The homepage is indexed under a promo URL.** Google chose `/?utm_source=serbito&utm_medium=promo&utm_campaign=seller_grid` as canonical and ignored `rel=canonical` to `/`. `/` itself is "unknown to Google". Cause: serbito.rs links to javi only with UTM URLs, on every page. Side effect: every organic visit would be attributed to the serbito promo campaign in GA4 and in the lead form.
3. **The page is too thin to rank for anything but the brand.** About 200 words. No "Viber", "SMS" or «praćenje pošiljke» in title/H1. No pricing, no how-it-works, no FAQ, no named operator.

**What works:** robots.txt and noindex rules from SERBITO-303 are correct. Private routes are disallowed. `/t/…` tracking pages stay crawlable and send `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` (also 404s). No private or tracking URL is known to Google. Lab performance is good (96).

**Site-wide facts, not bugs:** Cloudflare returns 403 to AI training bots by owner choice. The cross-promo footer (SERBITO-260) is present.

## Findings

### F1. Critical — Google indexes the English version of a Serbian landing

**Evidence:**
- `landing/index.html:370-379`: with no saved choice, the language comes from `navigator.language`; `setLang()` rewrites `<html lang>`, `<title>`, meta description, H1 and all text (`index.html:340-356`).
- Headless Chromium with Googlebot's UA, locale `en-US`: title "Javi — tell your customer when the parcel is on the way", H1 "Tell your customer when the parcel leaves…", `lang=en`. With `sr-RS`: Serbian. Googlebot renders with `en-US`.
- One URL serves three languages. `/en/`, `/ru/`, `/sr/` return 404. No hreflang anywhere. `og:locale` says `sr_RS`; the rendered page says English.

**Impact:** Serbian queries do not match the indexed text. English and Russian versions are not indexable either. The SERBITO-438 hreflang goal is blocked.

**Fix:**
- Ship three static files: `landing/index.html` (sr, default), `landing/en/index.html`, `landing/ru/index.html`. WhiteNoise (`WHITENOISE_INDEX_FILE = True`, `config/settings.py:163-164`) serves them as `/`, `/en/`, `/ru/`.
- Each file has its own title, description, H1, body, `<html lang>`, `og:locale`, self-canonical, and reciprocal `hreflang` sr / en / ru / x-default (→ `/`).
- Language buttons become links. Remove the auto-switch from `navigator.language`. Offer a small "English / Русский?" hint instead of rewriting the page.
- `landing/sitemap.xml`: add `/en/`, `/ru/`, `xhtml:link` alternates, `<lastmod>`. `config/test_seo.py` already requires the sitemap to match public landing pages; extend it to check hreflang reciprocity.
- `data-app-link` handler (`index.html:361-368`): derive the app language from the page URL, not localStorage.

### F2. High — the homepage is indexed under a serbito promo UTM URL

**Evidence:**
- URL Inspection: `/` unknown; the UTM URL is indexed with Google canonical = UTM URL, user canonical `/`.
- serbito.rs links to javi only with UTM URLs, on every page:
  - `serbito/ads/templates/ads/_footer_v2.html:77` — `?utm_source=serbito&utm_medium=crosspromo&utm_campaign=footer` (site-wide footer, about 4,100 URLs in serbito's sitemap).
  - `serbito/ads/templates/ads/ad_list_items.html:72` — `?utm_source=serbito&utm_medium=promo&utm_campaign=seller_grid` (page 1 of each listing).
  - Live check of `serbito.rs/sr-cyrl/items/components/`: both links present, no clean link.
- Internal links from javi to `/` do not exist (no logo link).

**Impact:**
- SERP shows a tracking URL; signals split between URL variants.
- Analytics damage: an organic visitor lands on the UTM URL. GA4 records the visit as the serbito promo campaign, and the lead form copies `utm_source=serbito` into the lead (`index.html:381-389`). Organic search becomes invisible in the data that SERBITO-438 needs.

**Fix:**
- serbito repo: link to `https://javi.serbito.rs/` without UTM in `_footer_v2.html:77` and `ad_list_items.html:72`. GA4 still records the visit as referral `serbito.rs`. If the campaign split matters, add the campaign as a fragment (`/#from=seller_grid`) and read it in the landing script.
- javi: make the header logo (`index.html:164`) a link to `/` (and to `/en/`, `/ru/` per language).
- After deploy: request indexing of `/` in Search Console.
- Same pattern exists for gtd and poker in `_footer_v2.html:55,75-76`. gtd's `/` is indexed today, so there it is a risk, not a fault. Check poker in its own audit.

### F3. High — the landing does not answer a shop owner's questions or match seller queries

**Evidence:**
- Word count: about 194 (sr) / 222 (en) rendered words, including form labels and footer.
- Title «Javi — obavestite kupca kada paket stiže» and H1 «Obavestite kupca kada paket krene — i kada stiže»: no "Viber", "SMS", «obaveštenje o isporuci/dostavi», «praćenje pošiljke». "Viber/SMS" appears only in a feature paragraph and in `og:description`.
- No pricing. "Isprobaj besplatno" does not say what is free, for how long, or what one SMS/Viber message costs.
- No how-it-works with screenshots of the Viber message and the `/t/` status page.
- No FAQ (which couriers, how the customer phone is used, GDPR/ZZPL, integration with WooCommerce/Shopify/API).
- Who runs it: only "Napravio No Handoff" (LinkedIn link) in the cross-promo block. No company name, PIB, address, or business email. The only contact is a personal Gmail on `/privacy.html`.
- `/privacy.html` still says Javi is "in the interest-check phase — a simple page with a contact form". The live product has registration, an app and an API.
- The public API docs (`/api/docs/`) are a trust and integration signal, but the landing does not link to them.

**Impact:** the story in SERBITO-438 is "find javi and test it without a sales call". The page gives neither the search match nor the facts a shop needs to sign up alone.

**Fix (`landing/index.html` and the new `landing/en|ru/index.html`):**
- Title ≤ 60 chars, for example «Obaveštenje kupcu o isporuci preko Vibera i SMS-a | Javi».
- H1 with the main term, for example «Viber i SMS obaveštenje kupcu kada paket krene i stiže».
- Meta description ≤ 155 chars with "Viber", "SMS", «praćenje pošiljke», "besplatno".
- Add sections (≥ 500 words sr): how it works (3 steps + 2 screenshots), pricing (free tier / per-message cost), who it is for (webshops, couriers, Instagram shops), integrations and API (link `/api/docs/`), FAQ (5-6 questions), "Ko stoji iza Javija" (company, PIB, city, business email).
- Update `landing/privacy.html`: current product scope, named controller, business contact.

### F4. Medium — structured data is minimal

**Evidence:** one `WebSite` block (`index.html:50-59`) with a nested `Organization` named "Javi" (no logo, contact, sameAs). No `SoftwareApplication`/`Offer`. No JSON-LD on `/privacy.html`.
**Fix:** per-language `@graph`: `Organization` (No Handoff as publisher; logo; `sameAs` LinkedIn), `WebSite` with `inLanguage`, `WebPage`, `SoftwareApplication` (`applicationCategory: BusinessApplication`, `offers` with real price, `areaServed: RS`). Follow the gtd pattern from SERBITO-445.

### F5. Medium — "Javi" alone is not a rankable brand

**Evidence:** a search for "javi.serbito.rs" returns Spanish footballers; GSC query "javi" sits at position 11.
**Fix:** use a descriptor everywhere the brand appears: «Javi — Viber/SMS obaveštenja o isporuci» in title, `og:site_name`, schema `alternateName`, Viber business profile, directory listings.

### F6. Low

- No Serbian Cyrillic version. Some sellers search in Cyrillic («обавештење о достави»). Consider `/sr-cyrl/` after F1.
- `/index.html` answers 302 → `./` (WhiteNoise). Prefer 301 or leave it (no links point to it).
- Lab LCP 2.7 s; 73 KiB unused JS is gtag. Load gtag after consent or on idle.
- javi's own footer links to gtd/poker/serbito with UTM (`index.html:257-259`): same canonical risk for the sister sites, low weight (one page).
- `consent.js` cache lifetime 4 h (`cache-control: max-age=14400`); hash the file name and cache for a year.
- HSTS without `includeSubDomains`/`preload` (site-wide Cloudflare setting).

## Proposed tickets

Only Critical/High, plus one backlog item.

### 1. javi: Serbian shops find the Serbian landing in Google (Critical)

- **Why:** Google renders the landing in English (`navigator.language` = `en-US`). Serbian queries never match. English and Russian cannot be indexed separately; hreflang is impossible.
- **Do:**
  - Static pages `/` (sr), `/en/`, `/ru/` in `landing/`, each with its own title, description, H1, `lang`, canonical, `og:locale`.
  - Reciprocal hreflang sr/en/ru + x-default.
  - Language buttons become links; remove the automatic language switch.
  - Sitemap: 4 URLs (with privacy), `xhtml:link` alternates, `<lastmod>`. Extend `config/test_seo.py` for hreflang reciprocity.
  - Owner: resubmit the sitemap in Search Console; request indexing for `/`, `/en/`, `/ru/`.
- **Done when:** URL Inspection shows `/`, `/en/`, `/ru/` indexed; a render with `en-US` locale of `/` shows Serbian text; Search Console shows a sitemap download after the release.

### 2. javi: Google lists javi.serbito.rs/ and organic visits count as organic (High)

- **Why:** Google indexed `/?utm_source=serbito&utm_medium=promo&utm_campaign=seller_grid` instead of `/`. Organic visitors would be recorded as the serbito promo campaign in GA4 and in leads.
- **Do:**
  - serbito repo: clean javi links in `serbito/ads/templates/ads/_footer_v2.html:77` and `serbito/ads/templates/ads/ad_list_items.html:72` (no query string; optional `#from=…` fragment).
  - javi: logo links to the language root.
  - Owner: request indexing of `/` after deploy.
- **Done when:** URL Inspection of `/` shows Google canonical `https://javi.serbito.rs/`; Search Console pages report shows `/`, not a UTM URL.

### 3. javi: a shop owner learns price, setup and who runs Javi before signing up (High)

- **Why:** the story is "test it without a sales call". The landing has ~200 words, no pricing, no how-it-works, no operator identity, and no seller keywords (Viber, SMS, «obaveštenje o isporuci»).
- **Do:**
  - Title/H1/description with seller terms (see F3).
  - Sections: how it works with 2 screenshots, pricing, who it is for, integrations + link to `/api/docs/`, FAQ, "Ko stoji iza Javija" with company, PIB, city, business email.
  - Update `landing/privacy.html`: current product scope, named controller, business contact.
  - Same content in sr/en/ru (after ticket 1).
- **Done when:** sr landing ≥ 500 words; price and operator visible without scrolling past the second screen; title ≤ 60 chars and description ≤ 155 chars contain "Viber" and "SMS"; privacy page describes the live product.
