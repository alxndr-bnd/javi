## Top findings before the fix

1. Thin landing: ~190 words, no price, no how-it-works, no FAQ, no operator (SERBITO-461).
2. H1 and description lacked seller terms ("Viber", "SMS", "obaveštenja kupcima o dostavi").
3. No `Offer` and no FAQ markup; `og:title` kept the old wording.
4. Two equal CTAs in the hero (register and lead form); no trial message.
5. Header logo was not a link; feature cards used H2 for short labels.
6. No funnel measurement: GA4 only page views (consent-gated), no events, no server counts.

## Fixed in this branch

- Title = descriptor + search phrase (≤ 60), new H1, description with price and trial (≤ 155), per language.
- Sections: who it is for, how it works (3 steps), features, price 30 €/month + 30 days free
  (card payment "comes soon"), FAQ (6 Q&A), who runs Javi (repo facts only, owner TODO).
- JSON-LD: `Offer` (30 EUR, P1M) and `FAQPage` equal to the visible FAQ.
- One primary CTA in the hero with the trial line; lead form shortened (no volume select).
- Logo links to the language root; cards use H3; internal links to `/api/docs/` and `/privacy.html`.
- Funnel: server counts (landing views, sign-up form opens) + Shop/Delivery history; GA4 events;
  `manage.py funnel_report`.

## Still open

- Privacy page still describes the "validation phase" product — SERBITO-461.
