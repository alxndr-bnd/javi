Who Javi is for

For small shops in Serbia that deliver their own orders and have no parcel tracking system of their own.

  • Online shops with their own delivery: the customer knows when the parcel arrives and waits at home.
  • Restaurants, bakeries and florists that deliver to the door with their own courier.
  • Shops with their own website: delivery notifications go out automatically through the API.

How it works — 3 steps

  1. Add your deliveries

    Enter the customer's name, phone and address — by hand in the dashboard or automatically through the API from your online shop.

  2. Tap “Delivery started”

    The customer gets a Viber message (or an SMS) with the approximate arrival time at once. We compute the time from the route, with traffic.

  3. The customer tracks the parcel

    The link shows the status and the arrival time, no app needed. After the delivery the customer rates it in one tap.

Frequently asked questions

Does the customer have to install an app?

No. The customer gets a Viber message or an SMS with a link and opens the status in any browser.

What if the customer has no Viber?

Javi sends a Viber message first. If it cannot be delivered, the customer gets an SMS.

How much does Javi cost?

30 € per month per shop. The first 30 days are free and no card is needed. Card payment comes soon.

Can I connect my online shop?

Yes. Javi has a REST API and signed webhooks: create an order, mark it as started and get the tracking link. Docs: javi.serbito.rs/api/docs/.

Which numbers does Javi send to?

A new shop sends only to Serbian mobile numbers and has a lower daily limit until we verify it. After the check the normal limits apply.

What happens to customer data?

We delete the customer's name, phone and address 90 days after the delivery ends. The customer can unsubscribe from messages. Details are in the privacy policy.
